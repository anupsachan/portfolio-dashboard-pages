Setup 1 — Gap & Go

  • Scan: gap ≥ 4%, high volume, clear catalyst
  • Prefer: float < 20M, price $2–$25 (higher OK if very fast)
  • Window: 9:30–10:00 AM ET primary
  • Entry: break PM high · 1-min ORH · red-to-green above prior close
  • Stop: low of entry / pullback candle (tight)
  • Target: base hits — $0.25–$0.50/share or next half/whole dollar

Setup 2 — Micro pullback

  • Stock already ripping on news + scanner
  • One red 1-min candle (pause) — or bull flag on 10-sec chart
  • Entry: first candle that breaks high of pullback after volume returns
  • Stop: low of pullback candle — do not widen
  • Higher risk than waiting for 5-min setup — only on strongest names

Indicators (Ross Cameron)

  • 9 EMA — first pullback support
  • 20 EMA — deeper pullback support
  • VWAP — above = bullish; fail to reclaim = exit
  • Volume — must confirm breaks
